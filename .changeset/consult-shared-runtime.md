Add `consult`, a model-invoked supporting skill for read-only Claude and Codex collaboration, with a Python CLI runner, session continuity, retained diagnostics and offline process tests. The runner uses plain-text prompts and replies, infers the caller and current project, and continues a conversation when `--resume` is supplied. Optional `--provider claude|codex` overrides the default counterpart, supports same-provider consultation and remains fixed on follow-ups. The `code-review` Standards and Spec sub-agents each use an independent consultant alongside their own review and return one report per axis. The `implement` skill discusses its approach with a consultant and can reuse that conversation throughout implementation, including TDD. The `implement-spec` implementers consult on their ticket's approach using their own worktree, and its final fixes implementer consults on the review corrections. The `diagnosing-bugs` skill compares independently generated hypotheses after reproducing and minimising the bug. The `improve-codebase-architecture` exploration subagent compares independent assessments with the same scope, criteria and architectural guidance. The `to-tickets` skill reviews draft breakdowns involving shared changes, coordination between parallel tickets or staged migrations before user approval. The `to-spec` skill reviews its draft for contradictions, ambiguous requirements and gaps before publication, checking feedback against the conversation to preserve agreed decisions. The `wayfinder` skill reviews proposed decision tickets and dependencies before creating its map and tickets. Other engineering and productivity workflows keep their behavior.
