Use a separate AI agent for an independent assessment, advice, or both. You are the **host**; the separate agent is the **consultant**, running in its own session.

The consultant stays read-only and cannot make changes or run tests.

**IMPORTANT: The consultant's input is advisory. You decide which ideas or findings are relevant, useful and well-founded.**

Read [runtime.md](references/runtime.md) before starting a consultation. Continue the same conversation for follow-ups. Start a fresh conversation for a new assignment or an independent final review.

## How to use it

You can use the consultant in two modes, separately or together.

### 1. Independent assessment

Ask the consultant to carry out the same review, analysis or investigation you are performing. Provide the task details and relevant context, and have it reach its conclusions independently.

Complete your own assessment before reading the consultant's conclusions.

Compare its findings with your own. You and the consultant may reach different conclusions, and that can be an acceptable outcome. You can continue the same conversation to ask why it reached a conclusion, clarify a finding, or discuss a different interpretation.

### 2. Advice

Use the consultant to explore ideas, think through a problem or consider a decision. Share your current thinking, even if it is incomplete. It can suggest possibilities, offer another perspective or ask questions that help you clarify your own reasoning.

You can discuss its suggestions, challenge its reasoning or keep exploring an idea in the same conversation.
